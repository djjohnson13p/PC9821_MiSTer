FLIPPRO: 0xA4 display-page flip latch-timing probe (PC-9821)
page 0 = WHITE, page 1 = DARK BLUE. Watch the graphics screen; stdout is the report.
Each frame: 0xA4=0 at the VSYNC edge, then 0xA4=1 at the trial's offset.
SPLIT (white above / blue below) = the flip applied AT the write (a tear).
UNIFORM screen that switches blue->white once = the flip is latched at the frame boundary.
OFF = ARTIC ticks (307200 Hz) since the VSYNC edge; V = estimated vline; ST = 0xA0 byte.
PERIOD=5445
