EGC v1.3 HARDWARE FINGERPRINT NOTES
===================================

INFO is not an error.  INFO rows preserve behavior for which the project does
not yet claim one universal EGC model.

READ fingerprints include:
- normal CPU-visible reads with nonzero 04AC;
- two-word carry/state for phases 1,2,7,8,15;
- source-ahead and reverse direction;
- byte read/compare paths;
- compare versus shifter and transfer length;
- P selector versus retained FG.

WRITE fingerprints include:
- FG-under-BG / BG-under-FG write gating;
- PLOAD_W ordering and ACCESS interaction;
- byte ROP/PAT low/high lanes;
- OPE/SHIFT/LEN/ACCESS/FGBG writes in mid-stream;
- direct P with nonzero alignment;
- PAT and FG persistence across disable/enable.

On real hardware preserve the exact CSV words.  A result matching neither
candidate model is valuable and should not be normalized or discarded.
